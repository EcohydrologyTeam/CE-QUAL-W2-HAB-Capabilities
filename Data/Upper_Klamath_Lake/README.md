# Upper Klamath Lake In Situ Chlorophyll Data

This data set was prepared by Dr. Jodi Ryder, ERDC-EL-EPW, and delivered to the CE-QUAL-W2 HAB team to use for satellite data calibration on August 6, 2025.

The spreadsheet contains all of the surface chlorophyll data from the EPA water quality portal for Klamath Lake.
 
## Steps

1. Downloaded all the chlorophyll, biological, and pigment data for Oregon from 2011-2024
2. Sorted out anything deeper than 1m. If no depth was given, assume it was surface.
3. Selected anything that was chlorophyll. Not sorted for filtered or non-filtered.  
 
## Notes

- The location names are not completely exclusive, i.e., the same name might be used on multiple sets of very close Lat-Longs and or the same Lat-Longs may have slight drift in location name. 
- Investigated all the Oregon sites.
- Detroit Lake has no data in the water quality portal.
- Crescent Lake and Odell Lake have some data but not as much as Klamath. Therefore, Upper Klamath Lake will likely provide the best data for the calibration.
- There is also phycocyanin and pheophytin pigment data for consideration at a future date.
